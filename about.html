About the Author

Veerendrra K. Sharma

Executive, Business & Operations Leader · Author · Founder, Wisdom4Action

Experience that became a way of seeing.

Veerendrra K. Sharma is an executive, business and operations leader with more than 35 years of experience across leadership, workforce solutions, staffing, payroll, skill development and large-scale business operations.

Over the years, his work has taken him into many different organizational environments. Those experiences have shaped an observation that visible success rarely tells the whole story.

Decisions, relationships, expectations, trust, culture and the conversations that happen — or remain unspoken — often influence what happens beneath the surface.

These observations led to Beyond Success, a series exploring the less visible dimensions of professional and personal success.

The Author's Work

The Invisible Architecture

The Invisible Architecture, the first book in the Beyond Success series, is a novel about what happens when the success we build begins to make us question who we have become.

Through the series, Veerendrra explores leadership, work, identity, relationships, purpose and the human side of achievement.
